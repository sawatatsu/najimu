1. Operator and scope

SWAN (operator: TATSUYA SAWANOBORI, referred to below as “we”) sets out the following information-handling policy for the iOS app neim (“the app”). Contact: info@swn.jp.

2. Information handled on your device

Your surname, given-name readings, kanji candidates, the currently displayed reading, and other settings are saved in the app’s dedicated storage on your device. Kanji suggestions and name composition are processed on your device. The app has no feature that sends these inputs to our server. Only if you use “Share with Partner”, some information is placed in your iCloud as described in 2-2.

2-1. Sharing you choose

When you use iOS sharing, an image of the displayed name (surname, kanji spelling, and reading) is passed to the app or destination you select. This does not pass through our server. Information handling at the destination is governed by that service’s policies.

2-2. Share with Partner (iCloud)

Only when you send an invitation and the other person joins, the following information is placed in your own iCloud (Apple’s CloudKit) and shown in the other person’s neim.

  • Information placed: the list of reading-and-kanji pairs, the list of pairs marked with ♡, the data format version, and whether sharing is paused because the inviter’s plan has ended
  • Information not placed: your surname, unfinished states, and device name
  • Where it is stored and who sees it: the data is placed in a shared area within the inviter’s iCloud, and the invited person uses that area through sharing (each person changes only their own record). You can only view the other person’s ideas, and both of you can add ♡. You can connect with one person only.
  • We cannot view this data. It does not use our server, and we have no feature for receiving it. The data is stored and transmitted in accordance with Apple’s iCloud systems and policies.
  • Signing in to iCloud is required. When you send an invitation, the link is passed to the app you choose, and its handling beyond that point follows that service’s policies. An invitation sent to a person selected in Messages or Mail can be joined only by that person; an invitation sent through another app can be joined by anyone who has the link. After the first person joins, no one else can join.
  • Stopping sharing: with “Stop sharing” in the app, the inviter ends the share and the invited person leaves it. In either case, the other person’s ideas and ♡ disappear from the screen, and yours are no longer visible to them.

Unless you use “Share with Partner”, nothing is placed in iCloud.

2-3. Plan purchases and subscription status

Purchases, payments, and cancellations of the “Share with Partner” plans (monthly and yearly) are processed by Apple’s App Store. We do not receive your name, Apple ID, or payment method (such as card details).

To verify purchases, check whether you are subscribed, restore purchases, and analyze subscription status, we send the following information to RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), our subscription management provider, which processes and stores it on its servers.

  • Information sent: your App Store purchase history, plan status such as expiration, renewal, and lapse, information identifying the purchase, and an anonymous user ID issued by RevenueCat
  • Information not sent: your name, email address, advertising identifier, or anything you enter in the app such as your surname or name ideas

The anonymous user ID is not linked to information that identifies you and is not used for advertising-related tracking. See the RevenueCat privacy policy for details. Other handling of purchase information follows Apple’s policies.

3. Advertising, analytics, and accounts

The current version has no account registration with us (“Share with Partner” uses Apple’s iCloud), no advertising, and no user-tracking feature. We do not run analytics that record the screens you view or your actions. RevenueCat is used to process and analyze purchases and subscription status, as described in 2-3. The app does not request access to your camera, photos, location, or contacts.

4. Information received through inquiries

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address, message, and attachments. We use these to respond, investigate problems, and manage support records. Please do not send personal information that is unnecessary for the investigation, such as a child’s real name.

5. Third parties and service providers

Information about purchases and subscription status is handled by RevenueCat, our subscription management provider, as described in 2-3. We do not sell or provide inquiry information for advertising. When using providers such as email services to handle inquiries, we process information only to the extent necessary. We may disclose information when permitted by applicable law, including when required by law.

6. Retention and deletion

Name ideas remain on your device until you delete them. Deleting the app deletes its local data, although it may be restored from a device backup managed through your Apple settings. The app does not sync to the cloud through our server. If you have used “Share with Partner”, the information placed remains in your iCloud and is not removed by deleting the app. To delete it, stop sharing in “Share with Partner”, then delete the app’s data in iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage (your ideas shown on the other person’s screen disappear when you stop sharing). Inquiry information is retained for as long as needed to respond and maintain records, then deleted when no longer needed.

7. Apple, external links, and the official website

The App Store, device backups, and diagnostic information are governed by Apple’s systems and policies. We have not added our own diagnostic collection to the app. When you visit the official website or an external page, hosting providers may handle technical information such as IP addresses and access times. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security. We do not place our own analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies on this site. See the GitHub privacy statement for details.

7-1. The website’s name demo

The website lets you assign kanji to prepared names. Selected kanji, typeface, and background settings are retained only while the page is open and are not saved in browser storage. Reloading returns the demo to its initial state. We do not send these selections to our server or sync them with the app.

8. Security and requests

We use reasonable measures, including access restrictions, to manage inquiry information. For requests to access, correct, or delete information we hold, contact info@swn.jp. We verify identity and respond in accordance with applicable law. We cannot view or change names stored on your device or placed in your iCloud through “Share with Partner”.

9. Changes

We update this page when information handling changes. Significant changes will be announced on this page or in the app, depending on their nature.
