1. Operator and scope

SWAN (operator: TATSUYA SAWANOBORI, referred to below as “we”) sets out the following information-handling policy for the iOS app neim (“the app”). Contact: info@swn.jp.

2. Information handled on your device

Your surname, given-name readings, kanji candidates, the currently displayed reading, and other settings are saved in the app’s dedicated storage on your device. Kanji suggestions and name composition are processed on your device. The app has no feature that sends these inputs to our server.

2-1. Sharing you choose

When you use iOS sharing, an image of the displayed name (surname, kanji spelling, and reading) is passed to the app or destination you select. This does not pass through our server. Information handling at the destination is governed by that service’s policies.

3. Advertising, analytics, and accounts

The current version has no account registration, advertising, analytics SDK, or user-tracking feature. The app does not request access to your camera, photos, location, or contacts.

4. Information received through inquiries

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address, message, and attachments. We use these to respond, investigate problems, and manage support records. Please do not send personal information that is unnecessary for the investigation, such as a child’s real name.

5. Third parties and service providers

We do not sell or provide inquiry information for advertising. When using providers such as email services to handle inquiries, we process information only to the extent necessary. We may disclose information when permitted by applicable law, including when required by law.

6. Retention and deletion

Name ideas remain on your device until you delete them. Deleting the app deletes its local data, although it may be restored from a device backup managed through your Apple settings. The app has no independent cloud synchronization. Inquiry information is retained for as long as needed to respond and maintain records, then deleted when no longer needed.

7. Apple, external links, and the official website

The App Store, device backups, and diagnostic information are governed by Apple’s systems and policies. We have not added our own diagnostic collection to the app. When you visit the official website or an external page, hosting providers may handle technical information such as IP addresses and access times. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security. We do not place our own analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies on this site. See the GitHub privacy statement for details.

7-1. The website’s name demo

The website lets you assign kanji to prepared names. Selected kanji, typeface, and background settings are retained only while the page is open and are not saved in browser storage. Reloading returns the demo to its initial state. We do not send these selections to our server or sync them with the app.

8. Security and requests

We use reasonable measures, including access restrictions, to manage inquiry information. For requests to access, correct, or delete information we hold, contact info@swn.jp. We verify identity and respond in accordance with applicable law. We cannot view or change names stored only on your device.

9. Changes

We update this page when information handling changes. Significant changes will be announced on this page or in the app, depending on their nature.
