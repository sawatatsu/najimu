2. The service

The app helps you assign kanji to a name’s reading, inspect it alongside a surname, and save candidates. It also offers “Share with Partner” (using iCloud), which lets two people show each other their name ideas. Sending an invitation for “Share with Partner” is paid (see 2-1); all other features are free. There is no advertising and no account with us.

2-1. “Share with Partner” plans

To invite another person, you need to subscribe to the monthly plan (¥300 per month) or the yearly plan (¥2,400 per year). Both are auto-renewing subscriptions and the prices include tax. The invited person can join without subscribing. Prices and conditions are shown in the app and on the App Store, and may differ by country or region.

  • Free trial: first-time subscribers to either plan get one week free. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, charges begin when it ends.
  • Payment and renewal: payment is charged to your Apple Account. The subscription renews automatically at the same price and for the same period unless auto-renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Cancellation: you can cancel in iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions. After cancelling, you can keep using the plan until the end of the period you have paid for.
  • When a subscription ends: “Share with Partner” stops, and the other person’s ideas and ♡ are no longer shown on either screen. The sharing setup and the other person’s record remain in iCloud, and sharing with the same person resumes if you subscribe again. To end sharing completely, use “Stop sharing”.
  • Refunds: refunds follow Apple’s policies. You can request one from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Price changes: if prices change, we will notify you in accordance with Apple’s procedures.

For seller information required under Japanese law, see the commerce disclosure (Japanese).

3. Naming reference information

Readings and meanings are dictionary-based reference information. They are not exhaustive and do not guarantee actual use in personal names or acceptance of a kanji-reading combination in family-register filings. Check with the relevant municipality or filing office where needed. The app does not provide name-based fortune-telling or predictions of good or bad luck.

4. Your information and storage

We do not acquire rights to the name information you enter. Completed combinations are saved automatically, and changes to saved ideas are reflected. When you use “Share with Partner”, your name ideas (pairs of reading and kanji) and ♡ are shown in the neim of the person you invited or who invited you. You choose who to share with, and their ideas are shown to you in the same way. Please handle the other person’s information with care, for example by not disclosing their ideas to others. You can stop sharing at any time with “Stop sharing”. Data may be lost or stop being shown if you delete the app or device data, if a device fails, or depending on the state of iCloud. Keep your own record of information you need.
